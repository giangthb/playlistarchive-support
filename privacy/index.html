Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026. This policy applies to A&F Playlist version 2.0 and later.

No data collection

A&F Playlist does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. The developer does not collect personal information, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, playlist names, imported audio or playback history through the app.

Local app data

Imported audio and playlist metadata are stored in the app's private container on your device. Removing an imported track from the library deletes the app-owned copy. Removing the app through iOS may also remove its local data.

System file picker

You choose files through Apple's system file picker. A file provider such as iCloud Drive may process your request under its own terms before A&F Playlist receives the selected file. A&F Playlist does not operate a server and does not upload the imported copy to the developer.

Network services

A&F Playlist has no developer account service, remote catalog, streaming service, cloud sync or analytics endpoint. The app is designed to organize and play local files offline.
